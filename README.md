## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a unified view of organizational performance across multiple business functions.

The solution brings together **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain and Executive reporting** into one centralized analytics platform. It enables stakeholders to monitor profitability, evaluate customer and product performance, measure forecast accuracy, identify inventory risks and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides dedicated analytical views for different teams while maintaining consistent filters, benchmarks and performance indicators throughout the report.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project was to build a scalable business intelligence solution that could:

* Provide a consolidated view of organizational performance.
* Replace fragmented reporting with a centralized dashboard.
* Enable function-specific analysis for Finance, Sales, Marketing and Supply Chain teams.
* Compare actual performance against benchmarks, targets and previous-year results.
* Identify profitable and underperforming products, customers, markets and regions.
* Monitor forecast accuracy, net error and inventory risk.
* Present executive-level insights for strategic decision-making.
* Allow users to move from high-level KPIs to detailed analytical breakdowns.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Net Sales reached approximately $3.74 billion**, remaining around 1.86% below the displayed benchmark.

2. **Gross Margin stood at 38.08%**, indicating a slight decline against the benchmark.

3. **Net Profit remained negative at -13.98%**, highlighting the need to control operational expenses and improve product-level profitability.

4. **Forecast Accuracy improved to 81.17%**, compared with 80.21% in the previous year.

5. **Absolute forecasting error decreased considerably**, indicating an improvement in overall forecasting performance.

6. Revenue is concentrated in one major business division, which contributes more than 60% of total revenue.

7. Retailers represent the dominant sales channel, contributing more than 70% of channel revenue.

8. Several high-revenue product segments continue to show negative net profit, suggesting that revenue growth is not consistently translating into profitability.

9. Forecasting risk varies significantly across customers and segments, creating both Excess Inventory and Out-of-Stock exposure.

10. A relatively small group of customers and products contributes a significant share of total revenue, creating opportunities for focused account and product strategies.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the insights generated through the dashboard, the following actions are recommended:

* Review the pricing and cost structure of high-revenue but loss-making products.
* Investigate operational expenses contributing to the negative Net Profit percentage.
* Prioritize high-value customers with strong Gross Margin contribution.
* Develop corrective action plans for customers with consistently low forecast accuracy.
* Improve demand-planning processes for segments exposed to Excess Inventory.
* Strengthen stock availability planning for products facing Out-of-Stock risk.
* Reduce revenue concentration risk by expanding secondary business divisions and channels.
* Monitor customer and product profitability alongside revenue rather than focusing only on sales growth.
* Use rolling forecast reviews to identify forecasting deviations earlier.
* Establish function-specific KPI ownership across Finance, Sales, Marketing and Supply Chain teams.
